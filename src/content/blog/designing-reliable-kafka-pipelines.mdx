Enterprise event systems rarely fail because Kafka is unavailable. They fail because the surrounding workflow is under-specified.

## The Pattern That Worked for Me

In high-volume compensation and healthcare pipelines, reliability came from combining three layers:

1. Deterministic sequencing for dependent events.
2. Multi-level retries with clear terminal handling.
3. Traceable observability for every state transition.

## Practical Rules

- Never mix business retry logic with transport retry logic.
- Make idempotency explicit at write boundaries.
- Build replay tooling before your first production incident.

## Outcome

When these patterns were introduced in production workflows, we improved consistency and reduced incident recovery time substantially.
